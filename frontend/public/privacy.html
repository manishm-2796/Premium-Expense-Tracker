Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Premium Expense Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and an encrypted hash of your password.

Financial Data: We securely store your expense records, amounts, categories, and descriptions to provide you with the tracking service.

Camera and Images: Our "Scan Receipt" feature uses your device's camera. The receipt images you capture are temporarily processed by a secure third-party OCR service to extract text (amount and date). We do not permanently store these images.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain the App, improve user experience, and securely sync your financial data across your devices.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/SSL.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@premiumexpensetracker.app.
